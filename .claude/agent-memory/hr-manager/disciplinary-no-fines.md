---
name: Lao disciplinary ladder has no monetary fines
description: The late-deduction ladder's fines/half-salary may exceed what Lao law allows as discipline; unconfirmed, needs a Lao labour lawyer
---

Lao Labour Law 43/NA sets a disciplinary ladder of non-monetary measures:
education/verbal warning -> written warning -> (transfer / suspension) -> dismissal.
It does **not** list monetary fines as a disciplinary measure, and wages are
protected (employer generally may only deduct for tax, SSO, court orders, and
genuine unworked time).

Family Studio's punctuality ladder imposes: 10,000 LAK per started late hour (from
the 4th late day), a full unpaid day for >2h lateness, and **half the month's basic
at 15 late days**. The "no work, no pay" pieces (unpaid day, arguably the hourly
charge as pro-rata for time not worked) are defensible; the **half-salary penalty
is almost certainly an unlawful fine** — it removes pay for time that was worked.
