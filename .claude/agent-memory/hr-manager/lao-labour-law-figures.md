Verified via secondary sources (Sept 2026), not the gazette text itself:

- **Annual leave**: minimum **15 working days/year, paid**, after completing **1 year
  of continuous service** (Art. 49). Source: laos.acclime.com/guides/employment-laws,
  usemultiplier.com/laos/employment-laws.
- **Sick leave**: up to **30 days/year at full pay** for monthly-paid staff, with a
  medical certificate. Same sources.
- **Overtime**: monthly OT cap commonly cited as **30 hours/month**. Premia cited
  inconsistently across sources — one set: 150% normal working day, 200% weekly
  rest day, 300% public holiday; another: 150–200% weekday / 250–300% weekend.
  Exact percentages + night rates NOT definitively confirmed; check Art. 53–54 of
  the gazette or the MoLSW. (Family Studio pays OT as a flat staged amount, so this
  only matters if OT is ever recomputed from hours.)
- **Minimum wage**: **2,500,000 LAK/month**, in force since 1 Oct 2024, still
  unchanged in 2026. Sources: kpl.gov.la/En/detail.aspx?id=86305, gpa.net (Laos
  minimum monthly wage). Relevant as a floor for the half-salary rule: half of a
  basic below 5,000,000 LAK drops under the statutory minimum.
- **Probation**: cited as 30 days (general) / 60 days (skilled) / up to 90 (managerial)
  — not load-bearing for payroll, low confidence.
