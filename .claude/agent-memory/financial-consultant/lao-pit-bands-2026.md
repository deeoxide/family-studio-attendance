# Lao personal income tax — monthly salary bands

New Law on Income Tax No. 88/NA, published in the Official Gazette 19 June 2026,
**effective 1 July 2026**, replacing Law No. 67/NA (2019) and its amendments.

| Monthly taxable income (LAK) | Marginal rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 2,500,000 | 0% |
| 2,500,000 – 5,000,000 | 5% |
| 5,000,000 – 15,000,000 | 10% |
| 15,000,000 – 25,000,000 | 15% |
| 25,000,000 – 65,000,000 | 20% |
| over 65,000,000 | 25% |

- Cumulative tax at band tops: 0 / 125,000 / 1,125,000 / 2,625,000 / 10,625,000.
- **No upper cap** — the 25% band runs to infinity.
- Exempt threshold (2,500,000) is now pegged to the statutory minimum wage, so it
  can move when the minimum wage moves. Re-verify the linkage each year.
- Overtime pay is PIT-exempt when the employee's monthly salary does not exceed
  LAK 3,000,000 (was 2,000,000 under the old law).

## Confidence / gaps
- 2.5M exempt threshold + new law No. 88/NA effective 1 Jul 2026 — PwC (Aug 2026)
  and VDB Loi alert concur.
- Some secondary trackers (TaxAtlas, ASEAN Briefing) still showed the old
  1,300,000 threshold on 2026-09-10 — not yet updated, do not rely on them.
- Band boundaries above 5M (15/20/25%) unchanged from the 2019 amended law.
- Could NOT find an English text of Law No. 88/NA itself — confirm with a Lao
  accountant / the Tax Department before finalising.
