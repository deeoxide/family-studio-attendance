# Lao social security (NSSF / LSSO)

- Employee contribution: **5.5%** of gross monthly remuneration.
- Employer contribution: **6.0%** (separate; not part of employee net pay).
- **Monthly wage ceiling: LAK 4,500,000.** Contributions are computed only on the
  first 4,500,000 of remuneration. Max employee contribution = 247,500 LAK/month;
  max employer = 270,000.
- Contributory base: gross remuneration (salary + allowances + OT + bonuses),
  capped at the ceiling. A minimum base tied to the statutory minimum wage also
  exists.
- Legal basis: Law on Social Security No. 34/NA (2013); LSSO relaunched in current
  form 3 June 2020. Ceiling figure of 4.5M repeated by PwC and Acclime.

## Confidence / gaps
- Rate 5.5% / 6% — high confidence, many concurring secondary sources.
- Ceiling 4,500,000 — secondary sources only (PwC WWTS, Acclime). Could NOT find
