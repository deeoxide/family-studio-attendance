Attendance · Leave · Payroll

A minimal attendance app for a Lao workplace

Four tabs, one primary action per screen. Check-in is gated on a 10 metre geofence around the registered office. Leave balances and working-day counts follow the Labour Law and the official holiday list of the Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare. Payroll shows the payslip an employee actually needs to read: net pay, then the deductions behind it.

White ground ≈ 90% Gold accent ≈ 7% Ink ≈ 3%

The phone below is live — move the distance slider to leave and re-enter the geofence, check in and out, apply for leave, open a payslip. Copy is English; Lao localisation is a next step, not a guess I want to make for you.
