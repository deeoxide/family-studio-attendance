Family Studio · attendance, leave, payroll

One app, three roles, two languages

Check-in is gated on a 10 metre geofence read as a plain distance meter — no map to load, no map to misread. Clock in at 09:00 with a 30 minute grace, lunch 12:00 to 13:30, out at 18:00, Monday to Friday. Leave is annual, sick and personal, counted in working days against the holiday notice of the Ministry of Labour and Social Welfare. Managers approve; HR runs payroll and keeps the holiday list.

Move the “Late days” slider to see each threshold.
